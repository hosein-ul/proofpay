What a proof says.
And what it can’t.

The Trust Model is on every review screen and in Settings. It is plain on purpose.

The proof guarantees

Verified on-chain by the escrow
  • The source returned this response. Over TLS, unmodified, at this time.
  • A registered attestor signed it. The signature is checked against the contract’s list.
  • It belongs to this task and this payee. Replays are rejected.

It does not guarantee

Decide these before you fund
  • That the source is telling the truth. A dashboard can be wrong.
  • That the worker did the work. Only that the source reports the condition.
  • Anything in Simulation. Simulated proofs are synthetic and labelled.
