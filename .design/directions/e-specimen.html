Four security features.
Four steps.

Like a banknote, each step has one feature that is easy to check and hard to fake. The escrow only ever checks the last two.

01 · Serial

Define

A source, a metric, a threshold, a date. The rule is hashed into a serial the proof must carry.

02 · Watermark

Lock

Funds in escrow are the watermark: always there, never in the way.

03 · Microprint

Prove

Everything is printed too small to read, except the one window the worker opens.

04 · Seal

Settle

The escrow checks the seal: signature, binding, replay and window. Then it pays.

What the seal says.
And what it cannot.

A proof shows the source returned this response. It does not show the source is right, or that the worker did the work.

The proof guarantees

Checked on-chain by the escrow
  • The source returned this response, over TLS, unmodified.
  • A registered attestor signed it. Checked against the contract’s list.
  • It belongs to this task and payee. Replays are rejected.

It does not guarantee

Decide these before you fund
  • That the source is truthful. A dashboard can be wrong.
  • That the worker did the work. Only that the source reports it.
  • Anything in Simulation. Simulated proofs are synthetic and labelled.
